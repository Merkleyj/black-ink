Questions, answered

Is it really free?

Yes — budgets, debt payoff, investments, CSV import and cross-device sync are free forever. The only paid feature is automatic bank syncing (Black Ink Plus), because each connected account costs us money to keep live.

Do you see my bank login or card number?

Never. Bank connections happen on Plaid's secure pages and payments happen on Stripe's — your credentials go directly to them. Black Ink only ever receives transaction data you've authorized.

What happens if I cancel Plus?

Your bank connections disconnect and everything already imported stays yours, editable manually. Nothing is deleted, and the free features keep working exactly as before.

Is my data sold or used for ads?

No. There are no ads, no trackers, and no data sales — the privacy policy commits to it in writing. You pay with money (or not at all), never with your data.

What devices does it work on?

Any modern browser. It installs like an app on your phone and desktop, works offline, and has a dedicated full-screen mode for a TV or wall display.
